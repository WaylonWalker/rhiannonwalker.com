Everything you read under this post was wrote during a time of fear, uncertainty, and trying to be the best mom/wife I could be through the choas.  Who am I kidding, I'm still trying.

Life has been going pretty great though and I truly have no complaints. Other than my body needing a day of rest, while my brain has an entire list of things I want to accomplish. 

Both kids are healthy, thriving, and fighting with each other through their teenage years. And we're only at 11 and 13. God knows what's coming next.  There's never an easy phase with kids, but I keep hoping. 

I have taken on many volunteer opportunities as my job.  Building sets/props for PAPAS musicals and other performances coming out on top.  Photography will always come out next as donating Newborn and Family sessions bring me so much joy. 

Them there's the never ending house design and remodeling, but that's homeownership.  Crazy we've owned this home since 2022.  It feels like yesterday we signed those papers.  When I look at the listing photos vs what we've changed...I'm glad we changed.   

Small family update.  

Ayla has now been in 6 different shows and is joining competition show choir.  This will be a whole nother adventure, but I'm looking forward to it. With that, I'm doing some photoshoots to raise money, but plan on keeping it to friends and family.  I'll always continue keeping my prices affordable, because I believe everyone deserves beautiful photos. 

Wyatt has grown so much and is about to pass me in height.  He's been going on long bike adventures with Waylon periodically.  Playing with some school friends on the computer, and always surprisingly me with his ability to create various games and websites.  He does use AI, but to get it to model exactly what he's thinking it the future of AI in the workplace.  He has a wonderful Special Ed team this year that understand both the struggles at home and our overall goal of having a functional  adult once school is done.   I'm proud of the hurdles he over comes on the daily, but truly has the most loving heart.  What I can't get over is how deep his voice has gotten.  If it's dark and I don't see him enter the room, his "mama" scares the shit out of me. 

Waylon is the same amazing supportive best friend I could ask for.  We have our moments, but they usually end within an hour of them starting and we move on.  We live creating sets to, but unfortunately due to the kids offer have to split up.  Since I'm available during the day it usual means I am the one heading down.  I don't mind it, but I miss him by my side.  I know he wants to be there too which makes it hard.  Work wise there's  been so many changes that I know have gotten under his skin, but I'm sure it will all work out.  I really like his new boss, and I'm feeling confident she'll take care of him. I'm always amazed at what he is able to do between work and his home projects.  I mean look at this website..it's all him!  No one makes me laugh and feel so incredibly loved more than Waylon.  We just celebrated 20 years of being together.  I can't wait to see what the future brings.

Finally me
  I've been working out at Ferrell's 5 days a week for over a year now.  It's incredible the things I'm able to do both on and off the mat.  I'm so proud of myself for all my hard work.  I feel more involved with the kids than I previously. Going from bed rest to getting shit down will do that. Medically.  I'm okay.  I go to therapy weekly.  Take my meds to manage my symptoms which allow me to be up getting stuff done.  I do have some items that are acting up like my iron leading to infusions.  My endometriosis is acting up and we have a surgery scheduled for January.  But my Cancer is just chilling in the background and getting my shots every 3 weeks.  It can stay that way please. 

 My relationship with my faith has changed and Ayla and I have been attending church most Sundays.  I may not agree with the extreme sides of Christianity, but the basics are right up my alley. 

Definitely live a very different life mentally than I did through a lot of those other posts. But don't worry, the chaos is still the same. I will always be remodeling my house in the spur of the moment and volunteering for way more than I should. We are in the teenage years, so buckle up. Waylon and I are not going anywhere, and at my core I will never change.
